# AI Lab OSS Field Guide

[Open the public guide](https://rohan-patnaik.github.io/ai-lab-oss-field-guide/)

Prepared for Rohan Patnaik. Company-menu revision: **19 September 2026 IST**. Original repository/history measurements: 18 September; new rows and issue audits carry their own timestamps.

- **30 established company/project groups; 89 implementation repositories.** Three screened repos per group except Unsloth, which has two. Cookbooks, example/notebook collections and content catalogs are excluded.
- **Six issue slots per company across its repos:** two LOW, two MEDIUM and two HIGH. **93 conditional leads across 40 repositories** survived screening; five groups have the full mix. The other 87 requested slots remain explicit gaps.
- Every issue option explains the failure, proposed fix, approximate changed LOC including tests, and remaining validation/approval gates.
- Contribution-history audit: 47 external PRs, 14 merged, with individual closure reasons and uncertainty.
- Repo policy/CLA/DCO/generation checks, external merged share/latency/backlog, a separate six-PR starter portfolio with fallbacks, and a twelve-week plan.

## Verification and limits

Opened and visually checked in a real browser at phone (390px), laptop (1366px), and desktop (1920px) widths, including themes, filtering, disclosures, navigation, and the new issue/LOC cards and tables. Build and local anchor/data checks passed. Live deployment is separately verified after publication.

External share among merged PRs is **not an acceptance probability**. Affiliation, historic employment and unlinked work remain uncertain. A lead is not a reproduced bug, accepted design, reservation or guaranteed merge. The review includes 301 individual issue audits; claims, ready patches and existing fixes were excluded even when unassigned. Bounded discovery is not proof no other issue exists. Re-check availability within hours before claiming or coding.

LOC ranges are editorial additions-plus-deletions estimates including tests; generated/lockfile/vendor churn is excluded. Full upstream/native/GPU/Windows/cloud validation was not run. Two narrow local harness checks are documented. The starter portfolio has fallback classification/ownership gates.

No upstream issue comments, issues, PRs or CLA signatures were submitted by this research task.
